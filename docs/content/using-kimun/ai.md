# AI assistants

You can let an AI assistant read and write your notes. There are two ways. Pick the one that fits your AI tool.

- Use the [MCP server](@/using-kimun/ai-mcp-server.md) with a desktop AI app or an editor, such as Claude Desktop, Zed, or Cursor.
- Use the [CLI skill](@/using-kimun/ai-skills.md) with a coding assistant in a terminal, such as Claude Code, Codex, or Gemini CLI.

Both can search, read, create, and edit notes. Both back up old text before they change it, so you can undo a mistake. See [Backups](@/using-kimun/cli.md#backups).

## Semantic search and Ask

Kimün can also use AI inside the apps. The optional [Kimün server](@/using-kimun/server.md) finds notes by meaning and answers questions from your notes. You do not need it to use Kimün.
