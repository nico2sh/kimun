## New view, new feature
Faceted view! A consolidated view that list all notes from a search together, one after the other, and offers filters to narrow the view to the elements that match the filter, based on labels, links and sections (from markdown headers). This help you narrow the context of large notes or queries.

## Quality of life features
Additionally to double clicking in links to follow them, now you can Ctrl+click (or Alt in MacOs) to do the same.
Frontmatters are collapsed in the view, and you can expand them with a click of a mouse, or moving the cursor inside.

## Right Clicks
You can now right click over a link for more options, such as open in a new tab or in a split.
