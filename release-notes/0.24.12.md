## Even a zen-er view
You can now hide the rail to have a clearer, more distraction-free view. Also if the vertical rail in the left side of the screen annoys you, you can now move it to the right side, or even at the top or the bottom if you prefer a horizontal rail.

## Custom fonts
You can set your own font in the text editor, in preferences, display, you will have available the system mono fonts.

## Auto-update
Auto-update now updates the app in place and restarts it, so you don't have to manually download it.

## Paste on MacOS
Fixed a bug where Cmd <clipboard actions> weren't captured, and only control was working.
