## Design Context

### Users
Writers and thinkers who keep plain-text notes in a personal vault. They read long stretches of text at a desk. The facet view is a reading and filtering tool: it shows parts of one note, or of a collection of notes, narrowed by tags, links, headings and search.

### Brand Personality
Calm, quiet, focused. The chrome stays out of the way and the text is the main thing. Motion is rare and small.

### Aesthetic Direction
Native desktop app (GPUI and gpui-kit), not a web page. Light and dark themes both follow the user's theme file. Colors come from the theme, never from literals. Review work against the gpui-kit design guides first, and use the impeccable principles for type, color, space, hierarchy and interaction. Skip the web-only rules (CSS, container queries, web fonts).

### Design Principles
1. Text first. Anything that is not the user's text is quieter than the text.
2. Structure by space and weight before lines and boxes.
3. Every control says what it does by its shape and place, not only by a tooltip.
4. Everything works from the keyboard, with a visible focus ring that is never clipped.
5. Derive colors and sizes from the theme and from shared constants. No literals.
